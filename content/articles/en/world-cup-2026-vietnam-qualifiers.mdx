The **2026 FIFA World Cup** will be the first edition featuring **48 teams**, hosted across the **United States, Canada and Mexico** from June to July 2026. For Vietnamese football, this represents a historic opportunity — the **national team** are fighting to qualify for the final Asian round and write a new chapter in the country's sporting history.

After reaching the Asian Cup quarter-finals in 2023, expectations for Vietnam have never been higher. Head coach **Kim Sang-sik** — the South Korean who previously led the U23 side — was appointed with a clear mission: take Vietnam through World Cup qualifying for the first time.

## Qualifying context

AFC has been allocated **8 direct spots** and **1 play-off place** for World Cup 2026. The final qualifying round features 18 teams in three groups of six, playing home-and-away round-robin fixtures. The top two in each group qualify directly; the two best third-placed teams enter a play-off.

Vietnam are in **Group B** alongside **Japan**, **Australia**, **Saudi Arabia**, **Indonesia** and **China** — widely labelled the "group of death" in Asian qualifying.

## Squad and playing style

Kim Sang-sik has built a squad balancing experience and youth:

- **Nguyen Tien Linh** — main striker with 15 qualifying goals, strong hold-up play and finishing
- **Nguyen Quang Hai** — creative midfielder, set-piece specialist and playmaker
- **Do Duy Manh** — defensive pillar with extensive Asian competition experience
- **Nguyen Hoang Duc** — box-to-box midfielder, recently transferred from Viettel to the J-League
- **Nguyen Van Toan** — first-choice goalkeeper in excellent V.League form

Preferred approach: **high pressing**, quick transitions and pace on both flanks. Kim Sang-sik emphasises tactical discipline and counter-attacking defensive structure.

## Challenges and opportunities

**Challenges:** Japan and Australia are Asia's elite. Saudi Arabia play a physically demanding style. Indonesia are improving rapidly with naturalised players. China will fight hard at home.

**Opportunities:** Home advantage at My Dinh Stadium, where fans create intense pressure on opponents. A rejuvenated squad with V.League players in peak form. Asian Cup 2023 experience helps players handle big-stage pressure.

> "Our target is to get out of the group and reach the play-offs. One match at a time." — **Kim Sang-sik**

## Vietnamese fans and World Cup 2026

World Cup 2026 holds special meaning for Vietnamese supporters. The expanded 48-team format increases opportunities for developing nations. If Vietnam qualify, they would become one of the first Southeast Asian nations to reach a World Cup.

Cafes, restaurants and viewing centres across Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang are preparing big screens for every qualifier. The hashtag **#Vietnam2026** has already generated millions of social media interactions.

## Training camp

The national team will assemble at **PVF** (Pho Thong Football Youth Training Centre) from August 2026. Kim Sang-sik plans to call up 28 players — roughly 70% from V.League and 30% based abroad or in regional leagues.
